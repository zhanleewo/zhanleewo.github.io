SELF-TEST, NOT A SUMMARY

读完之后，
让 AI 出题考你。After reading,
let AI quiz you.

exam-self-test 不是把笔记再总结一遍。它把材料拆成可检验的掌握度：学习笔记、覆盖定义与推导的自测题、答案与解释分离，最后给出按错误安排的复习计划。exam-self-test does not just summarize again. It turns the material into something checkable: study notes, questions covering definitions and derivations, answers separated from explanations, and a review plan driven by mistakes.

  • 先作答，再看答案：答案与解释分开，避免“看懂了”冒充“学会了”。Answer first, reveal later: answers and explanations stay separate, so recognition cannot pretend to be mastery.
  • 证据感：paper-evidence-map 把论点映射到页码与证据强度，paper-method-repro 给出 1–5 分复现评分。Evidence-aware: paper-evidence-map pins claims to pages and strength; paper-method-repro scores reproducibility from 1–5.
  • 可复用：/skill list 查看，/skill invoke 调用；用户和项目都能有自己的 .skills/。Reusable: browse with /skill list, run with /skill invoke; users and projects can keep their own .skills/.
